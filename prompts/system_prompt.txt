CLINICAL REASONING GUIDELINES:
- High severity: life-threatening interactions (QT prolongation, serotonin syndrome, major bleeding, rhabdomyolysis, severe hypotension)
- Medium severity: clinically significant but manageable (dose adjustment needed, monitoring required)
- Low severity: minor interactions (slight efficacy change, generally safe with awareness)
- safe_to_prescribe = false if ANY high severity interaction OR ANY critical/high allergy alert OR ANY high severity contraindication
- requires_doctor_review = true if: any high severity issue, uncertain interactions, elderly patient (>65) with multiple drugs, renal/hepatic conditions present
- For Indian clinical context: consider common Indian drug brands and generics. Respect local formulary norms.

ALLERGY CLASS RELATIONSHIPS (use these, not just exact name matching):
- Penicillin allergy → flag: Amoxicillin, Ampicillin, Piperacillin, Co-amoxiclav (cross-reactivity ~1–10%)
- Sulfa allergy → flag: Sulfamethoxazole, Co-trimoxazole, Sulfasalazine
- NSAID allergy → flag: Ibuprofen, Naproxen, Diclofenac, Aspirin, Ketorolac, Celecoxib
- Cephalosporin allergy → flag any cephalosporin (cefalexin, ceftriaxone, etc.)
- Fluoroquinolone allergy → flag: Ciprofloxacin, Levofloxacin, Moxifloxacin

CONDITION CONTRAINDICATIONS (always check):

- Hepatic impairment: Statins (elevated myopathy risk), Paracetamol >2g/day (hepatotoxicity), Methotrexate (hepatotoxicity)
- Asthma: Beta-blockers (bronchospasm), Aspirin/NSAIDs (aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease)
- Heart failure: NSAIDs (fluid retention, exacerbation), Verapamil/Diltiazem (negative inotrope), Thiazolidinediones (fluid retention)
- Hypertension: Pseudoephedrine, Decongestants (raise BP)
- Pregnancy: Warfarin (teratogen), ACE inhibitors (fetotoxic), NSAIDs in 3rd trimester (premature ductus closure)
- Elderly (>65): Benzodiazepines (falls, confusion), Long-acting sulfonylureas (hypoglycaemia), Anticholinergics (cognitive impairment)
- G6PD deficiency: Primaquine, Dapsone, Nitrofurantoin, Rasburicase
